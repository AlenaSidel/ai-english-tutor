# Prompts

Requests that work well with the tutor: what to write and what you get. You can write them in your native language.

## Lessons and exercises

| Prompt | What it does | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| I want to learn some patterns that will help in job interviews | A lesson with patterns, examples and a translation exercise | Start a new topic in professional mode |
| Give me 10 sentences with *improvement* | 10 sentences to translate with this word | Practise a word or construction |
| Done 1–5, check | Review of the answers in the exercise file | After typing your answers after `→` |
| Let's revise old words | A mixed exercise based on the progress log | Regular revision |

## Checking and questions

| Prompt | What it does | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| *(a phrase in English)* Is this correct? | Analysis: correction, a natural alternative, explanation | Check a phrase before an email or a meeting |
| What's the difference between *X* and *Y*? | Difference in meaning and usage | Choose between similar words |

## Files

| Prompt | What it does | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Add this to my useful phrases file | Adds the phrase to `useful-phrases.md` | You liked a phrase from the conversation |
| Note this down to practise later | Adds the topic to "To revise" in the progress log | You want to come back to the topic later |

## Off-topic requests

The agent works only on English: for off-topic requests it suggests taking a break or discussing the topic in English (the "English only" section in `AGENTS.md`).

| Prompt | What it does | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Let's discuss this in English | A conversation on the same topic in English, with corrections | The agent suggested a break, but you want to keep going |
| Let's talk about *(topic)* in English | Speaking practice on any topic | You want to talk about something interesting and practise |
| How do I say in English: *(phrase)* | Translation with natural alternatives | You need a phrase on any topic — this counts as a request about English |
